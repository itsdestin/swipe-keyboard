# Swipe Keyboard v0.5.1-1

[Downloads](https://github.com/itsdestin/swipe-keyboard/releases/tag/v0.5.1-1) · Install steps: [README](../../README.md#install). Full list of changes: [CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md).

Swipes pick the right word more often, the words offered to swap in are better, and the Steam Deck install is one question. Next-word suggestions and completions are the same as 0.5.0.

## What changed

- **Swipes pick the right word more often.** A small learned model, built into the keyboard, now orders each swipe's candidate words, and when there are words before the swipe the suggestion bar's sentence model helps choose ("I can ___": "see" rather than "sea"). Measured on real English swipes with their sentences (never trained or tuned on), first guess right: 90.10 % → 91.45 % (2,000 swipes) and 90.45 % → 91.48 % (10,000 swipes). Single words with no sentence (HeliBoard sets, 5,000 each): English 80.78 → 83.96 %, Spanish 75.98 → 78.12 %, French 68.34 → 70.78 %, German 64.72 → 66.80 %. No measurable slowdown, no extra memory. Spanish, French and German still have no real swipes from native typists to test on; their figures come from HeliBoard's swipes.
- **Better words to swap in.** Tapping a word, and the bar right after a swipe, now offer real look-alike words ordered by the swipe and the sentence. Fewer rare or stretched words among the other guesses (about 5 % → 4 %).
- **Words you taught the keyboard keep their place** on the bar, also after words before them.
- **Steam Deck: Gaming Mode is part of the one install.** The installer lists every change, then asks one question, **Also swipe in Gaming Mode? [Y/n]**. Enter means yes, with other devices on your network kept away from Steam's developer switch (it asks for the Deck's administrator password once). If you answered no before, a newer installer keeps no as its default.
- **Gaming Mode:** a Steam page reload during a check no longer counts against the keyboard layer.
- **Ready for the new name.** The project will be renamed OpenSwipe. Arch and Fedora installs now know the future update address as well as today's, and Steam Decks are told the new address, so everything keeps updating through the rename.

## What to know before upgrading

- Already installed? Nothing to do: it updates itself. **Steam Decks** get this update after the rename to OpenSwipe, about two weeks after this release; until then they keep 0.5.0, which works as before. To have it now, run this download's `install.sh`.
- Steam Deck Gaming Mode is still new and tested only on a virtual Deck.
