# Swipe Keyboard v0.5.0-1 (unreleased)

**Not published yet.** Draft notes for the next release; the owner publishes it once everything is complete, tested
and verified. Full list of changes: [CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md), section "0.5.0 (unreleased)".

## New in 0.5.0

- **Our own swipe engine, no FUTO code or models.** Swipe recognition, suggestions and learning are now entirely the
  project's own, open work (models trained and released by Destin Moss via youcoded.ai, 2026).
- **Plasma 6.9 ready.** One package carries the keyboard for Plasma 6.7/6.8 and for Plasma 6.9's rebuilt keyboard and
  starts the right one; a Plasma 6.9 upgrade is never blocked.
- **Better suggestions** in English, Spanish, French and German (open language files and small neural next-word models),
  **tap a word to swap it**, **texting words** (wyd, lmao, jaja, mdr...), and **learning from your typing** (off by default).
- **Apostrophes from the sentence:** "they were" / "we're going", "its" / "it's" are picked by the words around them.
- **Capital letters:** "I", "I'm", names, places, days and months come out capitalised in English, Spanish and French.
- **Licences:** MIT where possible; KDE's GPL choice only for the two keyboard add-on layers.

Also in this release, from 0.4.1: automatic signed updates on every platform, Gaming Mode on Steam's stable client.
