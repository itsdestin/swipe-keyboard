# Swipe Keyboard v0.5.0-1

[Downloads](https://github.com/itsdestin/swipe-keyboard/releases/tag/v0.5.0-1) · Install steps: [README](../../README.md#install). Full list of changes: [CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md).

Swipe typing, word suggestions and spelling help for KDE Plasma's on-screen keyboard, in English, Spanish, French and German. Everything runs on your device.

## What changed

- **No FUTO code or models.** Swipe recognition, suggestions and learning are now entirely the project's own open work (models trained and released by Destin Moss via youcoded.ai, 2026). Only FUTO's public swipe data (MIT) is used, to train the swipe model.
- **Plasma 6.9 support.** One package carries the keyboard for Plasma 6.7/6.8 and for 6.9's rebuilt keyboard and starts the right one. The 6.9 version is tested against KDE's development code, not yet on a real Plasma 6.9 device. A Plasma upgrade is never blocked.
- **Better suggestions** in all four languages (open language files plus small neural next-word models), **tap a word to swap it**, **texting words** (wyd, lmao, jaja, mdr ...), **capital letters** ("I", names, places, days, months in English, Spanish and French) and **apostrophes from the sentence** ("we're" or "were").
- **Learning from your typing** (off by default).
- **Licences:** MIT where possible; KDE's GPL choice only for the two keyboard add-on layers.
- Also from 0.4.1: automatic signed updates on every platform; Gaming Mode on Steam's stable client.

## What to know before upgrading

- **Swipe typing is a few points less accurate than before in English, Spanish and German.** English, on 1,946 real swipes inside sentences: first guess right 91.5 % of the time (the FUTO engine used up to 0.4.1: 93.3 %), top 3 94.6 % (96.6 %). The other guesses after a swipe are real words far more often: stretched spellings, rare words and rare names fell from 44 % of them to 5 %. Real swipes without a sentence: Spanish 75.9 % (FUTO 77.7 %), German 64.7 % (69.7 %); French is better, 68.2 % (64.6 %). Suggestions are better. Measured on the final test build, 2026-10-24 (`bench/swipe-scoreboard.tsv`).
- **Suggestions vs Gboard:** ahead on every recorded measure in all four languages (Gboard 14.2, Android 35 emulator, learning off, tested 2026-10-22). Nothing is claimed about newer Gboard versions or Gboard with learning on.
- Bigger Spanish, French and German word lists (the measured way to lift their swipe accuracy) are still being tested and will come in a later update.
- Spanish, French and German next-word suggestions are early baselines; non-English swipe models are trained partly on synthetic swipes.
- Steam Deck Gaming Mode is still a preview, tested only on a virtual Deck.
