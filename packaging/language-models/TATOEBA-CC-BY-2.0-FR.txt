Source: https://tatoeba.org/en/downloads
The per-language sentence exports used to build these models are listed with
immutable URLs, SHA-256 digests, and byte sizes in packaging/assets.lock.json.
The sentences are adapted into deterministic, locale-specific word n-gram
models. The accompanying <language>-CONTRIBUTORS.txt files name every author
whose sentence ID was included in the training split. Tatoeba's Terms of Use
state that the BY condition requires citation of the sentence author's name:
https://tatoeba.org/en/terms_of_use

License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/
Tatoeba attribution guide: https://tatoeba.org/en/terms_of_use

The models are compact baseline corpus models trained on Tatoeba; they are not
claims of broad or region-specific language coverage. Spanish Spain and Mexico
share the same Spanish model.
